This page walks through the four parts of the Fabrico window you use most: the palette, the inspector, the toolbar and the camera. Each section shows a picture of the app and says what you do there.

The pictures on this page show the same frontend and engine the desktop app runs. A script takes them in a browser, so they change when the app does.
