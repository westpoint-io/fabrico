You can change a board while it runs, and what an edit does to the [items](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-item) already on a [belt](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-belt) depends on the edit. Cut the end of a belt that hands items to a machine and they wait. Cut the end a machine feeds and they keep going. Delete the belt, or the machine it is docked to, and they are gone for good, even after an undo.

On the board below, a [Source](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-source) on a timer puts an item on its belt every 8 [ticks](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-tick), and the belt carries each one to a Debug [Sink](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-sink). Scripted edits take the belt off the Sink on tick 80 and put it back on tick 140, then turn the Sink away from the belt on tick 220 and back on tick 280. Each time the Sink is cut off, items pile up at the end of the belt.

## Unlink and Rotate

Unlink takes one [docked](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-dock) end of a belt off its [port](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-port). Rotate turns a machine a quarter-turn, and a docked belt whose end the turned port no longer faces comes off. Either way the belt stays where it is, and so do the items on it. What they do next depends on which end came off.

When the belt's head, the end that hands items to a machine, comes off, the items have nowhere to go. They move up to the end of the belt and wait there. Turn the machine back, dock the head again, or undo the edit, and they move in. In the test, a belt carrying 3 items to a Debug Sink still carries all 3 after the Sink turns away, and the Sink takes all 3, in the order they were sent, once it turns back.

Checked by <code>turning_the_sink_away_holds_the_items_and_turning_it_back_delivers_them</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

Unlinking the head from the Sink holds the items the same way: all 3 wait on the belt, and an Undo of the Unlink delivers all 3.

Checked by <code>unlinking_the_head_holds_the_items_and_undo_delivers_them</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

When the belt's tail, the end a machine feeds, comes off, the items already on the belt keep moving. Unlinking the tail from the Source leaves all 3 items moving, and the Sink takes all 3.

Checked by <code>unlinking_the_tail_leaves_the_items_moving</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

Turning the Source away from the belt's tail does the same: all 3 items reach the Sink.

Checked by <code>turning_the_source_away_leaves_the_items_moving</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

On the board above, the belt carries 4 items when the Unlink takes effect on tick 80. The Sink takes nothing more until tick 141, and by then 12 items are waiting at the end of the belt. Once the Dock joins it again they move in, and by tick 160 the belt is back to 4. The turn on tick 220 does the same: the belt carries 5 items when the Sink turns away and 12 by tick 280, and the Sink starts taking them again on tick 281.

## Delete

Select a machine or a belt and press <Fact id="shortcut.delete" /> to delete it. Deleting a belt deletes the items on it. Deleting a machine also deletes the belts docked to it, and the items on those belts go with them. A deleted item doesn't turn up anywhere else later.

In the test, deleting the belt while it carries 3 items leaves no item anywhere on the board, and the Sink never takes one.

Checked by <code>deleting_the_belt_drops_its_items_and_undo_brings_it_back_empty</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

Deleting the Sink instead takes the belt with it, and again no item is left anywhere.

Checked by <code>deleting_the_sink_deletes_its_belt_and_items_and_undo_brings_the_belt_back_empty</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

The engine has its own test for the same rule: it deletes a belt while an item is crossing it, runs on past the tick the item would have arrived, and checks that the item never reaches the machine at the far end.

Checked by <code>mid_flow_delete_drops_items</code> in [crates/fabrico-core/tests/edit_merge.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-core/tests/edit_merge.rs).

There is no move in the app. To move a machine, delete it and place it again where you want it, then draw and dock its belts again, because the delete takes its docked belts and their items with it.

## The delete dialog

When you delete a machine whose docked belts carry items, the app asks first. The dialog says how many items are in the way, and nothing is deleted until you pick one of its three buttons.

Checked by <code>the guard appears and sends NO command before a choice</code> in [frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts).

Deleting a belt that carries items opens the same dialog, worded for the belt.

Checked by <code>a belt carrying items opens the guard with the belt body copy, and Delete anyway deletes the belt</code> in [frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts).

Cancel closes the dialog and deletes nothing, so the items stay where they are.

Checked by <code>Cancel issues NOTHING and closes the guard</code> in [frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts).

Drain first waits while the items leave, showing how many are left, and deletes once the count reaches 0.

Checked by <code>Drain first waits for the belt to clear, THEN issues one Delete (disc 1)</code> in [frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts).

Delete anyway deletes straight away, and the items go with the belt as described above.

Checked by <code>Delete anyway issues EXACTLY ONE Delete (disc 1)</code> in [frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/frontend/e2e/delete-guard.spec.ts).

## Undo

Undo takes back a delete. The machine and its belts come back, docked as they were, but the belts come back empty. The items they carried stay gone, because the history of edits records what is on the grid, not the items moving on it. Putting items back would make up a board that a [replay](../../reference/files/glossary/#term-replay) of the logs could not reproduce.

Checked by <code>a_belt_carrying_items_comes_back_empty_and_that_is_deliberate</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/undo_restores_a_bare_belt.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/undo_restores_a_bare_belt.rs).

In the test, an Undo after deleting the Sink brings back the Sink, both docks and the belt with no items on it, and the Sink never takes any of the 3 the belt was carrying.

Checked by <code>deleting_the_sink_deletes_its_belt_and_items_and_undo_brings_the_belt_back_empty</code> in [crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs](https://github.com/westpoint-io/fabrico/blob/main/crates/fabrico-command/tests/items_in_transit.rs).

On the board below, the same Source, belt and Sink run with a different script. The Sink is deleted on tick 80 while its belt carries 4 items, and the belt goes with it. An Undo on tick 160 brings back both, docked, with an empty belt, and new items start along it. A Redo on tick 240 deletes them again, and a second Undo on tick 300 brings them back.
