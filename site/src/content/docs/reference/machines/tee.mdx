A Tee copies each item that reaches it and sends one copy out each of its outputs. Use it when two parts of a board need the same item, such as a line that handles orders and a second line that keeps a record of them.

## When an output is busy

The Tee sends both copies on the same tick. If one output's belt has no room for its copy, that copy becomes a <Fact id="fault.BranchBusy" /> fault instead, and the Tee shows as faulted on that tick; the other output still gets its copy. The Tee waits only when every docked output is busy. The board above spaces its items so each belt always has room.

An output with nothing docked gets no copy, and the Tee carries on. The troubleshooting entry for [a BranchBusy fault](../../../troubleshooting/fault-reasons/#fault-branch-busy) says how to give a slow output room.
